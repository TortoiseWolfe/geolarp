Players are free, forever. There is no pay to play and no pay to win. The whole game is free, including unlimited characters and one free backup slot.

The rule behind that: a paid feature is allowed only if a free player standing in the same cell loses nothing by not having it, and nothing that is free now will ever move behind a paywall. Every encounter is the same for every player.

What a paid plan is for

  • Convenience: more backup slots with version history, syncing between devices, offline city map packs.
  • Cosmetics: sheet themes, dice skins, portrait frames.
  • Organization, for Story Editors: the game-master role. A Story Editor's standing is earned by running sessions and being vouched for by players, and the subscription pays for the toolkit only: party coordination, a quest-line builder and session tools. Editors shape story and organization, never the dice, difficulty or rewards.
