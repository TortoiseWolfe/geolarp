geoLARP is still in development, and prices aren't set. There is nothing to buy today, and nothing to pay to play.

The aim is a game where paying never changes what happens in the street: the same encounter, the same dice and the same rewards for every player in the same spot. Anything paid would be extras around the game, not the game.

When there is a price for anything, this page will say exactly what it is and what it covers.
