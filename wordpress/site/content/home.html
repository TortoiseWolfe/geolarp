geoLARP is a steampunk tabletop-style role-playing game played by walking. You go outside, and something is on the corner ahead of you: a wandering automaton, a tinker-trader, a smuggler's cache, a wayshrine, a steam-snare. Nobody placed it by hand. That patch of ground and today's date work out what is there, and it is the same for every player.

You play where you stand. There is no respawn point and no teleport. A session is about half a mile of real walking.

Where it stands

geoLARP is in development. The rules are written, the character sheet and the encounter engine exist, and field tests on real streets are coming. There is no beta to hand you today. This site will tell you plainly when that changes.
