The street is cut into cells

The world is divided into hexagonal cells, each 100 metres across. A cell's coordinates plus the date pick one thing that is in it: a monster, a trader, a cache, a shrine or a trap, with a difficulty on a ladder from trivial to heroic.

  • The same for everyone. The cell holds the same thing for you as for the player who walks past an hour later.
  • Fixed until the world turns over. Cells reseed at midnight UTC (Coordinated Universal Time, the world clock), which in Chattanooga is 7 or 8 PM local time.
  • Works offline. Nothing has to be fetched to know what a cell holds, so your phone can work it out with no signal.

Dice: a seven-sided die

Dice use the West End Games D6 rating code, played with a seven-sided die, the d7. A code like 3D+2 means roll three d7, add them up, then add 2. The 2 is a flat bonus, not rolled; three of them make one more die.

One die in every roll is the wild die. A 7 on it explodes: roll it again and add. A 1 on it brings a complication. Ordinary dice never explode.

Your character

A character has five attributes: Strength, Agility, Intellect, Spirit and Luck. They start with 15D to share out between them. There are 20 skills, which start at 7D and begin at their attribute. The sheet is your own file, kept in your browser, and you can export it to a file or a QR code.

Character Points: a second chance

You start with 5 Character Points. After you see a roll, and only if it missed, you can spend a point to add one extra ordinary die, or to reroll the whole pool. You can spend at most 2 points on a single roll. Wins pay points back by difficulty, up to 5 a day, and a loss costs only the chance: no damage, no death.

Where you are, and who knows it

geoLARP is for adults, played with other people on real streets. While you play, you share your exact location with the players and game masters you are playing with. No history of where you went is kept, and your location is never sold or used for advertising. If you do not want to share your location, do not play.
